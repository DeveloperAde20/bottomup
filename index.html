Our Mission

“

To connect and empower stakeholders to achieve sustainable community and economic development through the transformation of food systems.

It's a simple idea: no single actor — not a community, a government, or an investor alone — can transform a food system by itself. We build the connections that let them do it together.

Why Bottom Up Impact?

We're a trusted convener — not another intermediary.

We align vision, finance and innovation to co-create food systems that are resilient and inclusive. We bridge the gap between grassroots needs and institutional resources, so the solutions that get built are practical, scalable and community-driven — built from the bottom up, not handed down.

Our Services

Four ways we bridge communities and capital.

Convening & Partnerships

Multi-stakeholder dialogues and coalition building — bringing communities, governments and partners to the same table.

Finance & Brokerage

Connecting communities with funders, investors and public–private partnerships to unlock the capital transformation needs.

Infrastructure & Innovation

Deploying practical, climate-smart systems that hold up in the field — not just on paper.

Advisory & Capacity Building

Governance, project management and knowledge-sharing that stay with partners long after we've left the room.
