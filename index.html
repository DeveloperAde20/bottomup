The Core Idea

“

Stronger food systems need stronger connections.

Food-system challenges rarely exist in isolation. Reliable production needs infrastructure. Infrastructure needs energy and investment. Producers need access to markets. Markets need reliable supply. Communities need access to information, technology and opportunities. Bottom Up Impact works across these connections, bringing stakeholders and resources together to develop practical systems that work on the ground.

Our Role

We connect what needs to work together.

Bottom Up Impact serves as a bridge between communities, government and institutions, infrastructure and technology, finance and investment, and markets and businesses. Our role is not to own or directly provide every component — it's to bring the relevant actors, resources and capabilities together around a shared opportunity.

What We Do

From local opportunity to functioning food systems.

Six interconnected areas of work — not separate departments, but the pieces Bottom Up Impact brings together around each opportunity.

Stakeholder Convening & Governance

Creating platforms that align farmers, government, businesses, financiers, technology providers and other stakeholders around shared opportunities.

Productive Clean Energy

Deploying modular clean-energy systems for productive agricultural uses such as cold storage, irrigation and processing.

Digital Connectivity & Hubs

Connecting communities to digital services, information, training, data and market opportunities.

Value-Chain Strengthening

Improving aggregation, processing, cold storage and market access so producers can capture more value.

Finance & Blended Models

Connecting viable opportunities to grants, concessional finance, investment and revenue-based models.

Measurement, Learning & Scaling

Tracking results, learning from implementation and using evidence to strengthen and replicate viable models.

Our Approach

Start from the bottom. Build upward.

We begin with the realities of a place — its people, resources, needs, opportunities and existing systems. From there, we identify what is missing, what can be strengthened, and what needs to connect.

Understand → Identify → Connect → Design → Mobilise → Implement → Learn → Strengthen → Scale

Start with what exists. Understand what is needed. Identify what is possible. Connect what is required. Build upward.

Featured Projects

Building where the opportunity is.

A selection of the food-system opportunities Bottom Up Impact is developing on the ground.

ALFSI — Aquaponics for Livelihood & Food Security

Ibeju-Lekki, Lagos State. Aquaponics, food processing and cold-chain infrastructure, training and empowering 12 young women while establishing market linkages with hotels, supermarkets and restaurants.

Dagbaja Oriade — Cocoa Aggregation

Osun State. Strengthening cocoa aggregation and post-harvest infrastructure with the local farmers' cooperative, alongside solar power and improved rural connectivity.

Opialu — Cassava Last-Mile Systems

Benue State. A clean-energy cassava processing machine and last-mile infrastructure connecting local production to processing and markets.
